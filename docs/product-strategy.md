# Careerize product strategy

## Product thesis

Careerize should become a trusted career-discovery and pathway-planning platform for South African learners who are making early subject, study and first-work decisions without enough exposure to real work.

It should not become a generic job board, a black-box personality test, a replacement for counselling, or an automated hiring decision system. The strongest path is a learner-first discovery product that compounds into school, funder and employer pathway intelligence over time.

## Primary users

1. **Learners and school leavers** who need practical exposure to careers before committing money and years.
2. **Schools, NGOs and funders** who need scalable, responsible career guidance.
3. **Employers and pathway partners** who want earlier, more credible talent pipelines without exploiting or over-scoring young people.

## Problem

Many learners choose subjects, courses or first jobs from limited family exposure, social pressure, vague prestige and incomplete information. They often know job titles before they understand the day-to-day work, stress, tools, entry route and growth ceiling.

## UX principle: progressive disclosure

The learner-facing home should stay light and emotionally safe. Careerize should ask for a small choice first, then reveal deeper route detail, reality checks and research links based on that learner's answers. This follows the UI/UX Pro Max guidance pattern of using structured design decisions, responsive layouts, accessible hierarchy and context-specific content instead of overwhelming users with every module at once.

## Trust principles

- Show how route suggestions are produced.
- Use signals as guidance, not identity labels or suitability verdicts.
- Explain the worst part of each path alongside the best part.
- Keep learner agency and human counselling in the loop.
- Do not claim verified employment outcomes until integrations and evidence exist.
- Do not automate rejection, ranking for employers, or high-impact decisions without explicit governance.

## Commercial wedge

The initial wedge is a polished, low-friction learner discovery experience that schools, NGOs, sponsors and employers can fund. The long-term defensibility comes from localised pathway content, aggregate labour-market insight, learner progress data with consent, and trusted relationships with education and employer partners.

## 2030-ready behaviours to compound

- Learners save evidence of experiments, not just quiz answers.
- Schools see anonymised guidance gaps and pathway demand.
- Employers contribute realistic pathway content and entry experiments.
- The catalog improves through local evidence and cited occupational research, not generic global job descriptions.
- AI assists drafting and reflection, but every high-impact recommendation remains explainable and human-reviewable.
