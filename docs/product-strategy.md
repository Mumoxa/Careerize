# Careerize product strategy

## Product thesis

Careerize should become the trusted, independent career-intelligence platform for South African learners who are making subject, study, training and first-work decisions without enough exposure to real work.

The strongest product path is not a marketplace. It is a learner-first discovery and career reality product that compounds through deep career content, qualification mapping, transparent route signals and learner-owned saved profiles.

## Primary users

1. Learners, school leavers and early-career explorers.
2. Parents and guardians supporting career conversations.
3. Teachers, counsellors, youth workers, schools, NGOs and public-interest institutions.

Employers, recruiters and course providers are not primary users of the product and must not influence career guidance or route signals.

## Problem

Many learners choose subjects, courses or first jobs from limited family exposure, social pressure, vague prestige and incomplete information. They often know job titles before they understand the day-to-day work, stress, tools, entry route, costs, earning reality and growth ceiling.

## Product principles

- Show what the work actually looks like.
- Explain the worst parts as clearly as the best parts.
- Treat route suggestions as exploration signals, not verdicts.
- Save learner context so guidance becomes more useful over time.
- Keep the learner in control of the final choice.
- Keep career content editorially independent.
- Avoid commercial influence over career profiles or recommendations.

## Personalisation model

Careerize may use saved learner profile data to:

- Remember what the learner has already explored.
- Suggest careers they may not know exist.
- Compare careers against their saved subjects, interests, concerns and notes.
- Recommend small, reversible next experiments.
- Help the learner prepare better questions for parents, teachers, mentors or practitioners.

Careerize must not use personalisation to make hidden decisions, exclude routes, sell access to the learner, or imply that the system knows the learner better than they know themselves.

## Commercial boundary

Sustainable funding may come from schools, libraries, NGOs, government bodies, ethical grants, institutional licensing or clearly separated public-interest sponsorship. Funding must never change career content, recommendations, rankings or learner access to core career information.

## 2030-ready behaviours to compound

- Learners save evidence of exploration, not just quiz answers.
- Career profiles become deeper, more honest and more local over time.
- Qualification maps show direct, adjacent and surprising routes.
- The system explains why it suggests a route.
- The product helps learners ask better questions before committing years and money.
