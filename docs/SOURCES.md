## External public sources to add before publishing salary, demand or formal qualification claims

These are target categories, not yet loaded source records:

- Stats SA labour-market publications for employment/demand context.
- SAQA/NQF records for qualification framework mapping.
- DHET and official institution sources for public universities and TVET colleges.
- SETA sector skills plans and learnership/apprenticeship information.
- Verified employer/practitioner interviews with consent for real story content.

## Rule

No Careerize page should display salary numbers, demand strength, qualification eligibility or employment outcomes unless the underlying record has a source URL, access date and confidence score.
