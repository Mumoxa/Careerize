# Careerize strategy and repository scope

Careerize is an independent, free career-intelligence platform for learners. It explains real careers, routes into work, qualification options, daily work realities, stress, tools, environment, lifestyle impact, earnings reality, growth paths, worst parts and best parts.

Careerize may save learner-owned profiles, discovery answers, notes and route comparisons so users can return later and receive more personalised guidance.

Careerize must not become a job board, recruitment tool, CV database, employer access platform, course marketplace, payment platform, sponsor-led content product, personality-test product or black-box AI career advisor.

The learner is never the product. Career content is never for sale. Saved records exist only to help the learner return, reflect and receive better guidance.
